# Quality gates

G1 impact and scope are clear
G2 evidence contains no secrets or real sensitive data
G3 timeline is reproducible
G4 hypotheses are separated from facts
G5 checks and queries are recorded
G6 risk tier and approval are recorded
G7 mitigation has rollback
G8 reviewers are listed
G9 cross-repository order is clear
G10 incident or follow-up record is complete
